The on-chain reader for Terraform grain seals (TFS1 seals hidden in a drawing's grain). This page talks only to your wallet: no other server, no scripts from elsewhere. It sends exactly one transaction, the contract creation, with no ETH and no approvals. The contract has no owner and no admin: once deployed nobody, including you, can change or remove it.
